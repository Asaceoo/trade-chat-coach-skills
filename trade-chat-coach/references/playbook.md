# 场景剧本库

配套 `SKILL.md` 的 Step 5 使用。每个场景四段：**怎么识别 → 本次目标（承诺阶梯哪一级）→ 可复制英文话术 → 别做什么**。

话术可直接发送。方括号 `[X]` 处替换成真实数字——**不要留空、不要编造**。

## S1 客户说 "your price is too high"

**识别**：一句否定，但没有对比基准、没有目标价、不谈规格差异。这类客户多数情况下只是在执行采购流程的规定动作，不是真的要走。
**本次目标**：阶梯 1 → 2，拿到"对比基准"或"目标价"其中之一。

```
Thanks for being straight with me. So I'm comparing the same thing — could you
share the spec and quantity behind that price? Very often the gap sits in the
material grade, the certification, or the packing, and if it's something we can
adjust, I'd rather adjust it than lose the order.
```

如果客户已经有明确目标价，把话换成：

```
If you can tell me your target price and the quantity, I'll be straight with you
about whether we can reach it. And if we can't, I'll tell you what would have to
change — material, packing, or payment terms — instead of quietly cutting
something inside the product.
```

**别做**：立刻降价（一次让步会重新锚定他的预期）；空喊 "our quality is better"；写三段成本解释——他根本没读。

## S2 只问价、不给信息

**识别**：连续两三次只要价格，数量/规格/目的国一个都不给。
**本次目标**：阶梯 0 → 1，用三项信息换一个报价。**不要发全表。**

```
I can quote you today — I just need three things so the number doesn't change
later: quantity, destination port, and whether you need certification. Which
model are you looking at?
```

当对方要"完整价目表"时：

```
Our list has 60+ models and the price moves with quantity, so sending all of it
usually just wastes your time. Tell me the application and I'll send the two
models that actually fit, with real prices for your quantity.
```

**别做**：把整张价格表发出去（等于把自己的底价免费送给整个市场）；报完价就等——报价后面必须挂一个只有他能回答的问题。

## S3 已读不回（第 3 次跟进）

**识别**：前两次跟进（间隔 3 天、7 天）无回应。先分清"没看到"还是"不想回"：换渠道发一次（WhatsApp → 邮件）即可区分。
**本次目标**：用**新信息**换一次回复，而不是再问一次进度。

```
One update that may matter to you: we've moved [model] to a new mould, so at
500+ pcs the unit price is now [X] — about [Y]% below what I quoted in [month].
If the timing isn't right, no problem — just tell me when to check back.
```

第 4 次（把决定权交还给他，反而最容易收到回复）：

```
I don't want to keep filling your inbox, so I'll close this file for now. If the
project comes back to life, just reply on this thread and I'll pick it up within
a day.
```

**别做**：连发三条 `Any update?`；表达委屈或催促；把"没回复"直接当拒绝。

## S4 要免费样品

**识别**：要 free sample，还要你付运费，理由是 "after testing we'll place a big order"。
**本次目标**：把样品变成一次**低成本的承诺测试**——真买家会接受规则。

```
Samples are free — the courier isn't, unfortunately. Two options: (a) you cover
the freight, and I ship today with your logo printed so you can check the print
quality yourself; or (b) we invoice sample + freight and deduct it in full from
your first order. Which works better for you?
```

**别做**：无条件免费 + 包邮（筛不出真买家，还会被反复薅）；因为一条请求就断定对方是骗子（先验证，再判断）。

## S5 要独家代理

**识别**：还没有首单、甚至没下过样品单，就要求某国/某区域独家。
**本次目标**：把"给或不给"变成"用什么条件换"。

```
Exclusivity is possible, and I'd rather build it with you than promise it to
three people at once. What we ask for is a first-year volume commitment —
typically [X] units — plus a quarterly review. If we hit that together, I'll put
your territory in writing. Could you share your projected annual volume for the
region?
```

**别做**：一口答应（把市场锁死在一个没验证过的人手里）；生硬拒绝；给无条件的口头承诺。

## S6 要账期（OA / D/P / 远期 L/C）

**识别**：首单就要求赊销、账期或远期信用证。
**本次目标**：给的是**阶梯**，不是"给"或"不给"。

```
For a first order we work on 30% deposit / 70% against B/L copy — that's how we
keep the price where it is. After two or three orders we can look at D/P or open
account, and if you'd like we can explore Sinosure-covered terms. Would the
deposit model work for the trial order?
```

**别做**：首单全赊；在没核实对方资信的情况下给账期；只回一个 "No"。

## S7 迟迟不下单

**识别**：该谈的都谈完了，客户也认可，但就是不给决定。
**本次目标**：用一个**真实**的时间锚换一个决定或一个明确的时间点。

```
Two things I should flag while you decide: our production slot for [month] closes
on [date], and the carton price goes up from [date] — that second one comes from
our supplier, not from sales. If you can confirm the quantity by [date] I can
hold both. If you need more time, just tell me and I'll plan around it.
```

**别做**：编造假涨价、假库存、假"最后一个位"（被识破一次，信任归零）；反复问 "any news?"。

## S8 "你们是工厂还是贸易商？"

**识别**：客户直接问身份，通常在筛选供应商类型。
**本次目标**：诚实 + 转成价值，而不是绕。

```
We're a trading company with our own assembly line for [product] — so you get
factory-level QC on the parts that matter, plus one contact who handles export
docs, certification and shipping for you. If a factory-only policy is a hard
requirement, tell me and I'll be straight with you about whether we fit.
```

**别做**：谎称是工厂（一次工厂审核就崩，顺带毁掉口碑）。

## S9 砍 MOQ

**识别**：客户想先小批量试单，但低于你的起订量。
**本次目标**：用"首单特例"换承诺，并明确这只是首单。

```
Our MOQ is [X] because of how we order material, but I can do a trial run of [Y]
on two conditions: it ships as a mixed container with another order, and the unit
price reflects the smaller run. If the trial works, we go back to normal MOQ from
the second order.
```

**别做**：无条件破 MOQ（之后每一单都会被继续压）；为了凑小单偷换材料。

## S10 客户在多供应商之间比

**识别**：客户明显同时在谈几家，问题在几家之间来回试探。
**本次目标**：让自己成为"最容易合作的那一个"，而不是最便宜的那一个。

```
You're probably talking to a few suppliers — that's the right way to buy. Three
things you can use to compare us: technical questions answered within 24 hours,
documents complete on the first submission, and production photos sent before
shipment without you having to ask. Test us on any of those with your next
question.
```

**别做**：诋毁同行（客户会同时怀疑你）；只靠降价参战。

## S11 同行 / 中间商套价

**识别**：问得比终端还细，却不谈数量、不谈用途，或明显在收集多家报价。
**本次目标**：保护价格体系，同时不排除真需求。

```
Happy to work with you. Our pricing depends on the channel, so I need to know one
thing: are you buying for your own project, or reselling? If it's resale, tell me
the market and I'll quote with a proper margin structure instead of a retail
number.
```

**别做**：直接给底价；反过来把所有人都当同行防（会得罪真实的中间商客户，而中间商往往是大单来源）。

## S12 消失后回来与老客户返单

**识别**：沉默一段时间后突然恢复联系；或合作过的客户对返单价格有异议。
**本次目标**：不追问沉默，直接给新价值。

回来时：

```
Good to hear from you. Since we last spoke we've added [new option / certification],
and lead time is now [X] weeks instead of [Y]. Want me to update the quote with
current pricing and shipping?
```

老客户返单议价：

```
For a repeat order at [qty] I can hold last year's price. Below that quantity the
material price has moved [X]% since [date] — I'd rather show you that split than
quietly cut something inside the product.
```

**别做**：说"你怎么这么久没回我"；对老客户一次性大幅让价（会永久重设他的价格预期）。

## 附 A 跟进节奏模板

固定节奏，每次带**一条新信息**。没有新信息就不要发。

| 时点 | 由头 | 目标 |
|---|---|---|
| D0 | 首轮报价 + 3 个问题 | 拿到数量/目的国/交期任一 |
| D2 | 补一条技术或选型建议 | 让他回一句 |
| D7 | 新样品图 / 新认证 / 新交期 | 阶梯 +1 |
| D14 | 具体方案（配置对照、成本拆分）或样品提议 | 要一个承诺 |
| D21 | 收尾信（close the file） | 让他主动开口，或安心降级 |

**升级路径**（一直不回时）：换由头 → 换渠道（邮件↔WhatsApp↔电话）→ 换联系人（找技术或采购）→ 降级到低频池。
**放弃条件**：三次不同由头 + 一次收尾信仍无回应，且对方从未提供任何具体信息 → 归 P3 档，停止单独投入。

## 附 B 禁句替换表

| 别写 | 换成 |
|---|---|
| Dear friend / My friend | Hi [Name] / Dear [Name] |
| Hope you are fine | （直接说要事；或 "Hope the [project/trade show] went well."） |
| Kindly do the needful | Could you confirm X by [date]? |
| Our price is very competitive / best price | We're at [price] for [qty]; happy to compare spec by spec. |
| Please reply ASAP | Could you confirm by [date] so I can hold the slot? |
| Sorry to bother you again | （直接给新信息，不道歉） |
| Any update? / Any news? | One update from our side: [新信息]. |
| We are a professional manufacturer with 20 years… | We've been making [product] since [year]; here's a photo of the line. |

## 附 C 报价后必做的一件事

每份报价后面**必须挂一个只有客户能回答的问题**（数量、目的国、认证、到货时间任选其一）。

理由：报价本身不给对方任何回复的理由，问题给。没有这一句，报价单就是一张比价工具。
