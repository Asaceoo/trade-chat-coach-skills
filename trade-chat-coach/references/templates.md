# 完整跟进模板库（D0→D21 整件可抄）

配套 `SKILL.md` 的 Step 5 使用。**与散装话术的区别**：这里是"整封可直接发的信"，从主题行到落款。所有 `[ ]` 必须填真实数字，**不填就别发**。

## 0 使用纪律

1. 模板是骨架，不是免检产品——发之前对照诊断卡确认：判断没变？客户语言对？数字真实？
2. 客户说中文用微信版，说英文用邮件版；说其他语言，正文用 i18n-phrases.md 的短句骨架 + 本模板的结构。
3. 每条只做一件事：要么给信息、要么要一个确认。**别在一条消息里既报价又降价又催单。**

---

## 1 D0 首次回复（询盘进来当天）

**英文邮件版**
> **Subject:** Re: Your inquiry about [product] — quick questions for an accurate quote
>
> Dear [Name],
>
> Thank you for your inquiry about [product]. To give you a number you can actually use, may I confirm three things:
> 1. Quantity and target price range
> 2. Destination port and Incoterm (FOB / CIF / DDP)
> 3. Certification required (CE / RoHS / etc.)
>
> With those I'll send a firm quotation within [24] hours. If you're in a hurry, a 10-minute call works too — which do you prefer?
>
> Best regards,
> [Name] / [Company] / [WhatsApp + WeChat]

**中文微信版**
> [称呼]，收到您的询盘。为了一次报准，麻烦给三个数：数量、目的港、要不要认证。给了之后我 [24] 小时内出正式报价。您方便的话，也可以约个 10 分钟语音，更快。

> 别做：一上来就甩价目表；只回一句"谢谢关注"。

## 2 报价邮件（报价 + 一个必答题挂尾）

**Subject:** Quotation [No.] — [product] [qty] pcs — [port]
> Dear [Name],
>
> Please find our quotation [No.] attached. Highlights:
> - Unit price [X] USD/pc FOB [port] for [qty] pcs (valid until [date])
> - Lead time [X] days after deposit; arrival approx. [week]
> - [Certification / packing / warranty] included as specified
>
> To keep this quote unchanged, could you confirm by [date]? After that the price follows the [material / freight] movement.
>
> Best regards, [Name]

**中文微信版**
> 报价单 [No.] 给您：单价 [X]、交期 [X] 天（定金后）、有效期到 [日期]。材料/运费下月要动，[日期] 前确认就按这个数走。您看行不行？

> 别做：报价后面不加问题；报价有效期写"长期有效"。

## 3 PI 跟进邮件（附 PI + 只要求一个动作）

**Subject:** Proforma Invoice [No.] — [qty] pcs — action needed
> Dear [Name],
>
> Attached is Proforma Invoice [No.] for your confirmation: [qty] pcs at [X] USD/pc, FOB [port], payment 30% deposit / 70% against B/L copy. HS code and Incoterm are stated on the PI as your finance team requested.
>
> Two things from our side:
> 1. Sample [No.] shipped [date], tracking: [number]
> 2. We can start production right after the deposit — to keep your [date] arrival, we suggest confirming within this week.
>
> Let me know if anything on the PI needs adjusting.
>
> Best regards, [Name]

**中文微信版**
> PI 附件请查收（数量/单价/条款都写好了，HS 编码和贸易术语也按您财务要求标了）。样品 [日期] 已寄，单号 [xxx]。定金一到就排产——要赶 [日期] 到货，建议这周内确认。有任何要改的您说。

## 4 D2 补一条价值（他还没回）

**英文邮件**

> Dear [Name],
> Quick update: we've [option A / new certification / lead time improvement]. Nothing for you to do — just wanted you to have the latest before you decide. Happy to answer any question.
> Best, [Name]

**中文微信版**

## 6 D14 方案推进（要一个承诺）

**英文邮件**
> **Subject:** Option to move forward — [product] [qty]
> Dear [Name],
> We've prepared two options for you: A) [standard spec] at [X] — ships in [days]; B) [upgraded spec] at [Y] — ships in [days]. Both hold your [date] arrival if confirmed by [date].
> Which one fits better? If neither, tell me what's blocking — I'd rather fix the real obstacle than keep sending emails.
> Best, [Name]

**中文微信版**
> 两个方案您挑：A [标配] [价格]，[X] 天到；B [高配] [价格]，[Y] 天到。都能赶 [日期] 到货，但要在 [日期] 前定。哪个合适？都不合适的话，您直说卡在哪——我不想光发邮件。

## 7 D21 收尾信（把决定权交还）

**英文邮件**
> **Subject:** Closing this file for now — [product]
> Dear [Name],
> I won't keep filling your inbox. If this project comes back to life, just reply to this thread and I'll pick it up within a day — quote, sample and slot can be renewed quickly.
> Thanks for your time either way.
> Best, [Name]

**中文微信版**
> [称呼]，这个单子我先收起来，不再打扰。项目要是重新启动，回我一句就行，当天给您更新价格和排期。谢谢您这段时间的沟通。

## 8 电话脚本（第一次打给询盘客户，60–90 秒）

```
[15 秒] 开场+确认身份："您好，我是[公司]的[名字]，您之前询过[产品]，方便说 1 分钟吗？"
[30 秒] 给一条价值+问一个信息："我打来是想确认下数量/目的港，这样报价能一次到位；顺便说下，[一条新信息]。"
[15 秒] 收口："那您把[数量/规格]发我微信/邮件，我 2 小时内出报价。可以吗？"
[10 秒] 结束："好，等您消息。"
```
> 别做：电话里报价；电话里硬逼决定；对方说忙还继续讲。

## 9 视频会议议程模板（10 分钟，发给他之前先发议程）

```
1. 双方自我介绍 + 确认需求（2 min）
2. 规格与参数核对（3 min）——提前把要确认的点发给他
3. 报价/交期/付款三件事过一遍（3 min）
4. 下一步与时间点（2 min）——"本周出 PI / 下周寄样"
```
> 规则：会前发议程 = 专业感；会后 2 小时内发会议纪要 = 可信度（峰终定律）。

## 10 催单邮件（有真实时间锚时）

**Subject:** [product] — [material/slot] update before [date]
> Dear [Name],
> [Genuine reason: production slot closes / material price moves / freight rising] on [date]. If you confirm the order by [date], I can hold the current price and lead time. If you need more time, just say so and I'll plan around it — no pressure either way.
> Best, [Name]

**中文微信版**
> [称呼]，[真实原因：排产位/材料价/运费]到 [日期]。您在 [日期] 前确认，我按现在的价格和交期给您留位。需要再想想也直说，我按您的节奏来。

### 10.1 四场景"合作收尾"话术（第四轮小红书 2463 赞笔记，`research\dangdang_booklist_20261008.md` 同期采集）

核心立场：**好好说话 ≠ 卑微讨好**——同一件事，别用"卑微提醒版"，用"合作收尾版"。

**① 催付款**｜别：`Kindly reminder: payment is due...`（卑微催）
> Hope you're doing well! I'm circling back on invoice [No.] for [amount], due [date] per our terms. Could you confirm the expected payment date? Happy to resend anything you need.

**② 处理客诉**｜别：敷衍甩锅
> Thanks for sharing this — sorry we missed the mark. I've looped in the team and we're prioritizing this. I'll update you within [timeframe] with a fix.

**③ 推产品**｜别：硬广直推
> Noticed [your progress], reminded me how [our product] helped [similar client] get [result]. Fits your work — happy to share a case study!

**④ 答质疑**｜别：空口保证
> Fair question. Other clients saw [example/data] with this. Happy to connect you with our specialist if you'd like.

**红线**：③④ 中的案例与数据必须真实可查；来源笔记末尾为第三方工具软文，本技能不背书任何"背调神器"。

## 11 交期延误通知（高情商场景，越早越好）

**Subject:** Update on order [No.] — delivery timing
> Dear [Name],
> I owe you an update on order [No.]: [reason — be concrete, no excuses]. Impact: shipment moves from [date] to [date] (new ETA [date]). I've already [mitigation: expedited / partial shipment / backup option] and will keep you posted with photos at each stage.
> I know this affects your schedule; tell me if the new date creates a problem and I'll look at [alternative] for you.
> Best, [Name]

**中文微信版**
> [称呼]，订单 [No.] 要跟您坦白：因为 [具体原因，不找借口]，发货从 [日期] 延到 [日期]（预计到 [日期]）。我已经做了 [补救：加急/分批/备选方案]，每个节点给您发照片。知道这会打乱您的安排——新日期有问题的话您告诉我，我再想别的办法。

> 别做：拖到最后一天才说；只说"抱歉"不给新日期和补救；把责任全推给上游。

**Subject:** Reorder — [product] [qty]
> Dear [Name],
> Good to hear from you again. For a repeat order of [qty] I can hold last year's price. Below that, the material has moved [X]% since [date] — I'd rather show you the breakdown than quietly cut something inside the product.
> Want me to issue the PI today?
> Best, [Name]

**中文微信版**
> [称呼]，返单按 [数量] 我按去年价给您保。低于这个量，材料从 [日期] 涨了 [X]%——我把涨价拆给您看，不在产品里偷工减料。今天出 PI 吗？
